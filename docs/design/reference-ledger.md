# Reference Ledger

[Back to the implementation plan](../implementation-plan.md)

Every reference, homage, and meme in VetroMania is tracked here, with where it
appears and how risky it is. A reference that isn't in the ledger doesn't
ship.

**Rules:**

- Parody and transformation only. New art, new words, and our own names.
- Never copy melodies, lyrics, logos, sprites, sound effects, or anyone's
  likeness.
- Short quotes are riffed on, not reproduced wholesale in a way that trades on
  the original.

**Risk tags:**

| Tag | Meaning | Process |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **L** | Low: short phrases, genre mechanics, tropes, or clearly transformed parody | Ships after writer and director review |
| **M** | Medium: a recognizable character, product, trademark, or a real person's handle | Legal review before beta; stays transformed, with no logos or likenesses |
| **H** | High: music, lyrics, logos, exact character designs, or real likenesses | Never ships as-is. Rewritten or cut (see [Rejected](#rejected-and-rewritten)) |

This ledger is a production tool, not legal advice. Final clearance comes from
counsel during Phase 4.

Area codes are listed in [World and map](world-and-map.md#areas-of-planet-vetromania).

## Adding a reference

1. Add a row here with its placement and a risk tag *before* it goes into a
   build.
2. The writer and director review the L entries. M and H entries go to
   legal review in batches every milestone.
3. Keep the reference to a layer. The joke must still work for players who
   miss it (the grandma test in the
   [tone bible](story-and-tone.md#tone-bible)).
